About Us

CerebroLux was founded by Wits Alumni, Farayi Myambo and Kgothatso Skosana. They are not only passionate about imparting knowledge, but they want to see children fall in love with their education and produce outstanding results. CerebroLux works towards building a successful community by employing graduates and those in pursuit of degrees on an ad hoc basis to assist learners in achieving excellent results.
